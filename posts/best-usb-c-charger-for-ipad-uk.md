## FAQ

**Q: Do new iPads come with a charger?**
A: No. Apple's spec sheets list only a USB-C charge cable in the box for the iPad and the iPad Air, so the plug is something you now buy yourself. That is the whole reason this guide exists.

**Q: What wattage charger does an iPad need?**
A: Any USB-C Power Delivery charger will charge an iPad, and more watts is faster only up to what the tablet itself will draw. Apple publishes a hard figure for just one model, the iPad Pro (M5) at 60W or higher; for a standard iPad, an Air or a mini, something in the 20W to 35W range is plenty.

**Q: Is 20W enough for an iPad?**
A: For a standard iPad or an iPad mini, yes, at a steady pace. It is the entry point rather than the quickest option, and it cannot hit the fast-charge figure Apple quotes for the iPad Pro, which needs 60W.

**Q: Can one charger do my iPad and my iPhone?**
A: Yes. The 35W pick adds a USB-A port and the 65W pick has three ports, so either charges an iPad and a phone at once. If the iPhone is the main thing you are charging, our [iPhone charger guide](best-charger-for-iphone-17-uk.html) picks for that instead.

**Q: Will a 65W charger harm my iPad?**
A: No. The iPad and the charger agree the power between them over USB-C Power Delivery, and the tablet never draws more than it is built to take, so a 65W plug is as safe as a 20W one. A higher-wattage charger just reaches the iPad's own ceiling sooner.
