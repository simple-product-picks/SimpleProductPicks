## FAQ

**Q: Will these hubs work with the MacBook Air M1?**
A: Yes. Its Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports take USB-C hubs, so any of the three plugs into either port.

**Q: Can a USB-C hub charge my MacBook Air while it's plugged in?**
A: Yes, if the hub has a USB-C PD port for your charger, as the Anker 7-in-1 and the UGREEN do. Check the Anker 8-in-1's listing for its charging figure before you rely on it.

**Q: Do I need one with Ethernet?**
