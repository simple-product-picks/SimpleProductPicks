## FAQ

**Q: What does GaN mean and why does it matter?**
A: GaN (gallium nitride) lets chargers run cooler and smaller than older silicon designs, so a 100W brick stays compact enough to carry every day without sacrificing output.

**Q: Can a 100W GaN charger charge my laptop?**
A: A USB-C laptop whose own charger is rated 100W or less charges at full speed from a 100W port, so check the wattage printed on your laptop's charger first, as some high-performance machines ask for more than 100W. If a USB-C charger will not charge your laptop at all, our [laptop won't charge over USB-C guide](laptop-wont-charge-over-usb-c.html) runs through the port, wattage and cable checks in the order that finds the cause.

**Q: Do I need a special cable to get the full 100W?**
A: Yes, you need a USB-C to [USB-C cable](best-usb-c-cable-for-fast-charging.html) rated for 100W (5A) with the right standards; a thin or low-rated cable will cap your charging speed regardless of the charger.
