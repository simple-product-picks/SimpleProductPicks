## FAQ

**Q: Do I still need a USB-C to Lightning cable in 2026?**
A: Yes, if you are charging an iPhone 14 or earlier, a Lightning AirPods case, or an older iPad or accessory that still uses a Lightning port. The iPhone 15 and later switched to USB-C and use a USB-C to USB-C cable instead.

**Q: Does an MFi cable charge my iPhone faster?**
A: Not on its own. Apple says fast charging an older iPhone needs both a USB-C to Lightning cable and an 18W or higher USB-C adapter, and a 20W or higher adapter for the iPhone 12 and later. The cable is one half of the job, the plug is the other.

**Q: Why does my iPhone say "accessory may not be supported"?**
A: Apple says that alert can appear when an accessory is damaged, is not Apple certified, or when the port is dirty or damaged. Using an MFi certified cable removes the certification as a cause, so it is the safest place to start.
