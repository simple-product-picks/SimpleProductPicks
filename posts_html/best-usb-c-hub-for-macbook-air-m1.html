FAQ

Q: Will these hubs work with the MacBook Air M1? A: Yes. Its Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports take USB-C hubs, so any of the three plugs into either port.

Q: Can a USB-C hub charge my MacBook Air while it's plugged in? A: Yes, if the hub has a USB-C PD port for your charger: the UGREEN's listing title rates its PD at 100W. Check the two Ankers' listings for theirs before you rely on them.

Q: Do I need one with Ethernet? A: Only if Wi-Fi isn't enough where you work. If Ethernet is all you're missing and you already have a hub, a standalone USB-C Ethernet adapter adds the port on its own.
