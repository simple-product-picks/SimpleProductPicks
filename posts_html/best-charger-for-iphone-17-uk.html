FAQ

Q: Does the iPhone 17 need Apple's 40-watt charger? A: No. Macworld's conclusion was that "No matter what power adapter you use, your new iPhone 17 isn't going to draw more than 25-28 watts." Every plug here gives 30W or more.

Q: What charger does the iPhone Air need? A: Apple rates the Air at up to 50% in 30 minutes with a 20-watt adapter or higher, a lower bar than the iPhone 17's. All three picks clear it, so choose on ports instead of wattage.

Q: What wattage do I need for MagSafe on an iPhone 17? A: Apple quotes 50% in 30 minutes from a 30-watt adapter or higher paired with its MagSafe Charger, and rates MagSafe and Qi2 wireless at up to 25 watts on the iPhone 17 and up to 20 watts on the Air. All three picks meet or clear that 30-watt bar. If you're wondering how those two names differ, our Qi2 vs MagSafe explainer covers it.

Q: Will a more powerful charger damage my iPhone? A: No. USB-C Power Delivery is a negotiation, so the phone asks for what it wants and the charger holds the rest in reserve. Check the cable instead, since it can only pass what it is rated to carry; our USB-C cable guide covers that end.

Q: Can I keep using my old iPhone plug? A: Yes. A lower-wattage plug still charges an iPhone 17, just more slowly; it can't reach the 20-minute figure Apple quotes against a 40-watt adapter or higher.
