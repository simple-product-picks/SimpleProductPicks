FAQ

Q: What wattage charger does a standard iPad need? A: Its spec sheet doesn't name one. Apple says only "Charging via power adapter or USB-C to computer system", so any USB-C Power Delivery plug will charge it, and so will a laptop's USB-C port when you're away from a socket.

Q: Can one charger do my iPad and my iPhone? A: Yes, the two- and three-port picks here charge both at once. If the iPhone is the main thing you're charging, our iPhone charger guide picks for that instead.

Q: Will a 65W charger harm my iPad? A: No. The iPad and the charger agree the power between them over USB-C Power Delivery, and the tablet never draws more than it is built to take, so a 65W plug is as safe as a 20W one.
