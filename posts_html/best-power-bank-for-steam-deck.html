FAQ

Q: What wattage power bank do I need for a Steam Deck? A: 45W or more from one USB-C port, over USB Power Delivery: Valve lists the Deck's own charger as a "PD3.0 Type C power supply, 45W". A total shared across several ports doesn't tell you what one port gives, so read the per-port figure on the listing.

Q: Can I charge the Steam Deck while playing? A: Yes. A USB-C port that gives 45W or more matches Valve's own 45W charger, so the Deck charges in play just as it does from the wall socket.

Q: Can I take a Steam Deck power bank on a flight? A: Yes, in your carry-on bag, never the hold. Check the watt-hour figure printed on the bank against the limits in our power bank flight rules guide, which also covers the rules on using a power bank on board.
