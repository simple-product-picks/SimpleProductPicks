FAQ

Q: Are USB-C hubs any good, or do they cause problems? A: A decent one from an established brand is reliable for everyday display, charging and data. The catch is usually the laptop rather than the hub: a USB-C port has to support DisplayPort Alt Mode before any hub can push video out of it, and some budget laptops and tablets ship data-only ports. Check that first, then match the hub to what you'll actually ask of it. The other common snag is power rather than the port: a bus-powered hub shares one socket's budget across everything plugged into it, so if you plan to hang external drives or other hungry kit off it, our powered USB hub explainer covers when a mains-powered model is worth it.
